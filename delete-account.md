# How to delete your Sticker.fit account

18 August 2026

---

## From the app

This is the fast way, and the one we recommend, because the deletion happens right away:

1. Open Sticker.fit.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings**.
3. Open **Account** and choose **Delete account**.
4. Confirm. The app tells you beforehand what disappears and what doesn't.

## Without having the app

Write to `stickerfit.contact@gmail.com` **from the same email address you used to sign in with Google**, with the subject
"Delete my account". That is enough: it is what lets us check the account is yours.

If you can't write from that address, say so in the message along with your username —the one starting
with `@`— and we'll ask you for another way to verify it.

We reply within one month at most, which is the legal deadline, and in practice well before.

## What disappears

- Your profile: username, display name, bio and avatar.
- Your date of birth.
- Everything you published in the catalogue, including its files.
- Your followers and the people you followed.
- Your likes.
- Your activity notifications.
- The notification addresses of your devices.
- Your sign-in account: after this, signing in with Google creates a new, empty account.

## What doesn't disappear

- **The packs and stickers on your phone.** Deleting your account doesn't delete your material: it stays
  there and keeps working offline. If you also want to get rid of it, uninstall the app or delete them
  from inside it.
- **Premium, if you bought it.** It is tied to your Google account and not to your Sticker.fit account,
  so deleting this one neither takes it away nor refunds it: you still won't see ads.
- **What other people already downloaded.** Those are their files, on their phones, and there is no way
  to reach them.
- **Packs other people have published with your material.** Since version 5 of the terms, publishing in
  the catalogue lets everyone else reuse what is published inside their own packs (section 4.4). Your
  entry leaves the catalogue; theirs stay, because they were published when it was allowed.
- **Reports you may have filed**, which are kept without staying linked to you. Otherwise, deleting your
  account would be a way of withdrawing your own reports.
- **The download count of other people's packs**, which stops being linked to anyone.
- **Anything a legal obligation requires us to keep**, and only for as long as it lasts.

## How long it takes

From the app, it is immediate.

By email, however long we take to handle the message, with the one-month maximum above.
