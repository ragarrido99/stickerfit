# Sticker.fit privacy policy

Version 5 · 28 August 2026

---

## 1. Who processes your data

Raúl Garrido Rasillo, Maria Antonia Paris 7, 43204 Reus, España, hereinafter "we". Sticker.fit is an Android app for making stickers and sharing them.

For anything related to this policy, including the requests in section 9: `stickerfit.contact@gmail.com`.

## 2. The important part, on one page

- **Without an account, the app sends us nothing.** Making stickers, editing them, building packs,
  installing them in WhatsApp and sharing them happens entirely inside your phone. Your photos never
  leave it, and background removal runs on the device itself, without uploading the image anywhere.
- **Adding a pack to WhatsApp carries an ad, and therefore needs a connection.** It is the only part of
  the local half that does, and only since 17 August 2026: an ad appears each time a pack is added to
  WhatsApp, whether yours or from the catalogue. We still receive nothing; who collects data there is
  Google's advertising system, and section 5 says what. Sharing a sticker with any app still has no ads
  and no connection.
- **Browsing the catalogue doesn't require an account either.** When you browse or download from it, our
  service sees what any server sees of any visit, and records the fact of the download so it can count
  how many a pack has.
- **With an account the rest begins**: a public profile, what you publish, who you follow, what you like
  and the notifications you receive.
- **What you publish is public.** There is no way to publish to the catalogue "just for me": publishing
  is exactly making it visible to anyone, with or without an account.
- **You can delete your account from the app itself**, and also without having it installed. Section 9
  says what disappears and what doesn't.
- **If you buy premium, the payment is processed by Google Play and we see no payment details.** We
  don't even store the fact that you bought it: that lives in the store. Section 5.1 explains it.

## 3. What happens without an account

None of this reaches us:

| What | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| Your photos and the stickers you make with them | On your phone, in the app's private storage |
| Your packs and their content | Same |
| Your loose sticker library | Same |
| Your preferences: theme, language, whether you've seen the intro, whether you want notifications | Same |
| The history of which packs you installed in WhatsApp | Same |

Two details worth knowing, because they aren't obvious:

- **Android's backup may take that data.** If you have the system backup switched on, Android copies the
  app's files to your Google account. That happens between your phone and Google, doesn't go through us
  and you control it from your phone's settings. It is enabled on purpose: without it, changing phone
  would lose you packs that exist nowhere else.
- **When you install a pack in WhatsApp, WhatsApp receives its stickers.** From then on it is their
  content and their policy governs it, not this one. The same applies when you share a sticker with any
  other app.

## 4. What we process when you create an account

Signing in is optional and it exists so you can publish, follow people, like things, have a profile and
receive notifications.

**Identity.** You sign in with your Google account and we create no password. From that process our
identity provider stores your email address, the identifier Google assigns to your account and the basic
data Google supplies with it, such as your name and your Google profile picture. **We never see your
Google password**, neither we nor our provider.

**Profile.** A username you choose, a display name, an optional bio and an optional avatar. **All of that
is public**: it appears as the author of whatever you publish and anyone can see it.

**Date of birth.** Asked once and **used for two things**: checking you are at least 16 (section 8) and
deciding whether the ads served to you can be personalised (section 5). The date is stored, it is not
verified against any document, and **it is neither public nor ever leaves the server**: the app doesn't
receive it back, it only receives the answer to those two questions.

**The version of the terms you accepted**, so we know whether to ask you again when they change.

**What you publish.** The sticker and pack icon images, the name, the description, the category, the
tags, the emoji associated with each sticker and the accessibility text. Images are stored exactly as
you send them: **our server does not rescale, re-encode or generate thumbnails**.

**Community.** Who you follow and who follows you, and what you have liked.

**Activity.** The notifications you generate for other people and those others generate for you: that
someone followed you, that someone liked something of yours, and the download milestones of what you
published.

**Notifications on your phone.** If you turn notifications on, we store the address Google's messaging
service assigns to that installation of the app, so we can send you the notice. When you turn the switch
off, that address is deleted from our service.

**Moderation.** If you report something: what you reported, the reason you chose and the explanation you
write, if you write one. If you block someone, who you blocked. And if something of yours is reported,
the report and whatever is decided about it.

**Usage.** That a pack or sticker has been downloaded, with the date. If you are signed in, that download
stays linked to your account for the current day, which is what lets us avoid counting the same download
twice. **We don't profile your interests, we don't analyse your behaviour and we use no analytics
tools.**

**Service logs.** Our provider keeps the technical logs any server keeps —IP address, date, requested
path and response code— for a short period and for the sole purpose of running the service and detecting
abuse.

## 5. Advertising

**The ad appears in exactly one place: when adding a pack to WhatsApp.** It appears once for each pack
you add, whether you made it yourself or downloaded it from the catalogue, and there are no ads anywhere
else in the app. Downloading from the catalogue and saving a loose sticker carry no ad.

Ads are served by Google's advertising system, which to do so collects device identifiers and its own
technical data. That processing is done by Google under its own policies; we neither receive that data
nor give them anything of yours. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the
first time you will see a consent message where you can choose what you allow, and that choice can be
changed later from the app's settings.

**Ads are not personalised unless we know you are 18 or older.** That is the rule and not an exception:
by default a non-personalised ad is requested, and a personalised one is requested only if you have
created an account and the date of birth you declared says you are 18 or over. Since adding a pack to
WhatsApp doesn't require an account, **the normal case is the non-personalised ad**.

The app doesn't send your age or your date of birth to the advertising system: the only thing travelling
with the ad request is the flag saying it cannot be personalised.

**The ad is the condition for adding the pack**: if none is available, or if you close it before it
finishes, the pack is not added and you can try again. It is what sustains the cost of the app and the
catalogue.

**What that implies, plainly: adding a pack to WhatsApp needs a connection.** Making stickers, editing
them, building packs, your loose sticker library and sharing a sticker with any app keep working with no
network and no ads. Adding it to WhatsApp does not.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, not accepting the consent message means no ad can
be shown to you, and therefore neither can packs be added to WhatsApp nor content downloaded from the
catalogue. The rest of the app keeps working the same. **The other way out is premium**, which is what
the next part is about.

### 5.1 If you buy premium

There is a one-off payment that removes ads from the whole app; the terms are in section 10 of
the terms and conditions. What matters here is only what touches your data, and it is little:

- **With premium no ad is requested at all**, so Google's advertising system stops receiving anything
  from the app. It is the only way that happens.
- **The payment is processed by Google Play.** We neither see nor store card details, payment method or
  billing address. What reaches us is the money, through Google.
- **We don't store the fact that you bought it.** The entitlement lives in Google Play and the app asks
  your phone's store, not our service. That is why premium works without having an account with us,
  restores itself when you reinstall or change phone, and **deleting your Sticker.fit account doesn't
  take it away**.

Put another way: buying premium **creates no data about you in our service**. Whatever Google processes
on its side to charge you is governed by its own policies.

## 7. Who else sees this data

**What is public** is seen by anyone, with or without an account: your profile, what you publish, the
follower, like and download counters, and the list of who you follow and who follows you.

**What is not public** is not handed to anyone for their own purposes. It is processed on our behalf, and
only to provide the service:

| Who | For what |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase | Hosting of the database, of file storage and of authentication |
| Google | Signing in with a Google account |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) | Delivery of notifications to the phone |
| Google's advertising system | The ads in section 5 |
| Google Play | Charging for premium, if you buy it. The payment doesn't go through us |

**We don't sell personal data and we don't do advertising based on what you do inside the app.**

Apart from that, we may disclose information when a law or a competent authority requires it.

## 8. Minors

**You must be 16 to create an account.** The check is the date of birth you declare, unverified, which is
what is proportionate for a service this size.

It is a condition of the account and not of the app: making stickers, editing them and installing them in
WhatsApp requires no account, so that half can be used below that age. The only thing there that does
process data is the ad in section 5, and who processes it is Google.

**Anyone we don't know to be 18 or older is not served personalised ads**, and that includes anyone using
the app without an account, which is most people. It is set out in full in section 5.

If we learn of an account belonging to someone under 16, we remove it.

## 9. How long it is kept, and what happens when you delete your account

While the account exists, its data is kept.

**You can delete it from the app itself**, in Settings, and also without having it installed by writing to
`stickerfit.contact@gmail.com`. When you delete it:

- **Your profile, your date of birth, your catalogue publications and their files, your followers and
  followees, your likes, your notifications and your notification addresses disappear.**
- **The content on your phone does not disappear.** Your packs and stickers are still there: deleting the
  account is an identity operation, not a loss of material.
- **What other people already downloaded does not disappear.** Those are their files on their devices, and
  we have no way to reach them, nor should we.
- **Two things survive without your name**: reports you may have filed, which are kept without identifying
  who made them —otherwise deleting your account would be a way of withdrawing your own reports— and the
  download count of other people's packs, which stops being linked to anyone.
- **Whatever a legal obligation requires us to keep is kept**, and only for as long as that obligation
  lasts. In particular, if something of yours was under review as allegedly illegal, the material and the
  case file are kept for as long as necessary.

## 11. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, restriction of processing,
portability of what you have given us, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where the
basis is your consent, you can withdraw it whenever you want without affecting what was done before: the
notification switch is exactly that.

Write to `stickerfit.contact@gmail.com`. We reply within the period the law sets, which is one month.

Much of this doesn't need to be asked of us: the profile is edited from the app, notifications are turned
off with a switch, what you published is taken down whenever you want and the account is deleted from
Settings.

If you think we haven't done it right, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency
(www.aepd.es) or to the supervisory authority in your country.

## 12. Security

The service runs over encrypted connections. Access to data is limited by rules applied on the server
itself and not in the app, so they don't depend on the phone behaving as we expect. We don't look after
any password of yours, because there isn't one.

No service can promise nothing will go wrong. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will report it
to the supervisory authority and to you when the law requires it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If it changes, it is published here with its date, and previous versions remain accessible. When the
change is significant, notice is given inside the app.
