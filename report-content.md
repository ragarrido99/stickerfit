# Reporting content on Sticker.fit

18 August 2026

---

## From the app

This is the fastest way, and **you don't need an account**:

- On a pack or a sticker in the catalogue: open it and use **Report**.
- On a person: open their profile and use **Report**.

You pick a reason and can add an explanation of up to 280 characters. Every character is read by a
person, so short and specific really helps.

## Without the app

Write to `stickerfit.contact@gmail.com` with the subject "Content report" and tell us:

- **What the content is.** The name of the pack or sticker and who published it —their username, the one
  starting with `@`—, or the link if you have it. Without this we can't find it.
- **What's wrong with it.** In a sentence or two.
- **If you are acting as a rights holder**, tell us what right you hold and how to verify it.

**You don't have to identify yourself** to report content, except for a rights claim, where we do need
to know who is claiming.

**Don't attach the material.** Telling us which one it is, is enough; forwarding it means distributing
it again.

## What happens next

A person looks at it. Depending on what it is, the content may be hidden from the catalogue from the
first report while it is reviewed.

The deadlines and the criteria are in section 6 of
the [terms and conditions](terms.md), explained in plain language.

**A report is not a guarantee of removal**: the review may end with the content staying where it is. And
the other way round, there is no need to insist: one clear report is enough, and several reports from
the same person don't weigh more.

## If it's urgent

If what you saw involves a **risk to someone's life or safety**, also alert the authorities in your
country. We are not an emergency service and we are not watching the inbox around the clock.
